# Dashboards – Excel & Power BI

## Overview

After completing the data analysis, I created two dashboards to present the results in a simple and easy-to-understand way. 
One dashboard was created in **Microsoft Excel** and the other in **Power BI**.

The dashboards bring the important results together in one place so that the owner, manager and marketing team can quickly understand how the marketing activities are performing.

---

# 1. Microsoft Excel Dashboard

The Excel dashboard was created to give a quick summary of the complete marketing data.

It shows important numbers such as **Total Spend, Total Leads, Total Conversions, Total Revenue, Average ROI and Conversion Rate**.

It also shows:

* Conversions by city
* Marketing performance by channel
* Performance of different audience segments
* Top-performing campaigns
* Leads, conversions, revenue and spending

Filters for **Channel, Audience Segment, Source and City** were also added. These filters make it easier to look at a specific part of the data.

![](https://github.com/siddiqmulla/IM-Packaging-Marketing-Analytics/blob/main/Dashboards/excel.png)

### Dashboard Interpretation

The Excel dashboard gives a clear picture of the overall marketing performance.
The analysis shows **₹82,749.48 total marketing spend, 2,243 leads, 913 conversions and ₹42,623,226 revenue**.
WhatsApp generated the highest number of leads and conversions among the four channels. 
Kolhapur recorded the highest number of conversions among the analysed cities, while Packaging had the highest conversions among the audience segments. 
The top campaign table helps identify individual campaigns that generated higher conversions and revenue. 
Overall, the dashboard makes the analysis easier to understand without going through all the individual Excel sheets.

---

# 2. Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard was created to make the analysis more interactive.

It presents the main marketing KPIs at the top and uses charts and tables to show the performance of different channels, cities, weeks and audience segments.

The dashboard includes:

* CPL
* Total Spend
* Total Leads
* Total Revenue
* ROI
* Conversions by City
* Revenue by Week and Channel
* Conversions by Channel
* Channel and Audience Segment performance

Slicers for **Channel, Week, Audience Segment and Source** allow the user to filter the dashboard and view specific results.

![](https://github.com/siddiqmulla/IM-Packaging-Marketing-Analytics/blob/main/Dashboards/PowerBi.png)

### Dashboard Interpretation

The Power BI dashboard gives an interactive view of the marketing results. 
It shows **CPL of ₹36.89, spend of ₹82.75K, 2,243 leads, revenue of ₹42.62M and ROI of approximately 51,409%** based on the project's calculation. 
The charts make it easy to compare cities and marketing channels, while the weekly chart helps understand how revenue changed during the month. 
The channel performance table provides a more detailed view of leads, revenue and conversions across audience segments. 
The slicers allow the user to quickly change the view and analyse a particular channel, week, segment or source.

---

## Overall Dashboard Workflow

The complete process followed was:

**Data Collection → Python Cleaning & Analysis → SQL Analysis → Excel Analysis → Power BI Analysis → Dashboard → Business Insights**

The **Excel dashboard** provides a simple summary of the results, while the **Power BI dashboard** provides a more interactive way to explore the same marketing data. 
Together, they make the final analysis easier to understand and use for marketing decisions.
