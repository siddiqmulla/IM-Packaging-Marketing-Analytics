# Excel Marketing Analysis

## Overview

Microsoft Excel was used to organise, summarise and analyse the marketing data of IM Packaging. After the data was prepared, Excel was used to examine performance across marketing channels, geographical markets, audience segments and individual campaigns.

The analysis mainly focused on **Spend, Leads, Conversions and Revenue** to understand how different parts of the marketing activity were performing.

---

## 1. Data Preparation

The prepared marketing data was organised into a structured master dataset. The dataset contains campaign-level information such as date, campaign ID, channel, source, city, audience segment, impressions, clicks, website visits, leads, conversions, spend and revenue.

This master data was used as the base for the different Excel analyses.

## 2. Channel Analysis

Channel analysis was performed to compare the performance of **Social Media, Website, WhatsApp and YouTube**.

For each channel, the analysis summarised total spend, leads, conversions and revenue. This made it possible to compare the contribution of each marketing channel and identify channels generating stronger results.

## 3. City-wise Analysis

The city-wise analysis was used to understand marketing performance across the main geographical markets of IM Packaging: **Sangli, Kolhapur, Tasgaon, Goa, Hubli and Belgaum**.

Conversions, revenue and leads were summarised for each city. This provided a clear comparison of the different geographical markets.

## 4. Audience Segment Analysis

The audience segment analysis examined the major industries targeted by IM Packaging, including **Engineering, Food Processing, Manufacturing, Packaging and Pharmaceutical**.

The analysis compared leads, conversions, spend and revenue across these segments to understand which target industries were generating stronger marketing outcomes.

## 5. Campaign Analysis

Campaign-level analysis was performed to get a more detailed view of individual campaigns.

Campaign ID, channel, city, audience segment, conversions, revenue and spend were compared to identify campaigns that generated higher conversion and revenue results.

## 6. Excel Analysis Workflow

The overall Excel workflow followed the following process:

**Prepared Data → Master Dataset → PivotTables → Channel Analysis → City Analysis → Segment Analysis → Campaign Analysis → Business Insights**

Excel helped convert the prepared marketing data into structured summaries that could be easily compared and interpreted. The results from this analysis were later used as a foundation for dashboard development and further marketing analytics.
