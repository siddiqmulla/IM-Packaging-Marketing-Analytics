# Power BI – Marketing Analytics

## Overview

Power BI was used to turn the prepared IM Packaging marketing data into an interactive analytical dashboard. The main focus was to create useful KPIs and measures that help understand marketing spend, leads, conversions, revenue and campaign performance.

---

## 1. Total Spend

Total Spend was created to understand how much money was invested in the different digital marketing activities. This provides the basic cost figure needed to compare marketing investment with the leads, conversions and revenue generated.

![](https://github.com/siddiqmulla/IM-Packaging-Marketing-Analytics/blob/main/PowerBI/Screenshots/Total%20Spend.png)

---

## 2. Total Leads

Total Leads shows the overall number of potential customers generated through the marketing activities. This is important because leads represent the initial business opportunities created by the different marketing channels.

![](https://github.com/siddiqmulla/IM-Packaging-Marketing-Analytics/blob/main/PowerBI/Screenshots/Total%20Leads.png)

---

## 3. Total Conversions

Total Conversions measures the number of leads that progressed to successful conversions. This is one of the most important measures in the project because the analysis focuses on how effectively marketing activities generate actual business results.

![](https://github.com/siddiqmulla/IM-Packaging-Marketing-Analytics/blob/main/PowerBI/Screenshots/Total%20Conversions.png)

---

## 4. Total Revenue

Total Revenue represents the revenue generated from the analysed marketing activities. Comparing revenue with marketing expenditure helps understand the financial outcome of the campaigns.

![](https://github.com/siddiqmulla/IM-Packaging-Marketing-Analytics/blob/main/PowerBI/Screenshots/Total%20Revenue.png)

---

## 5. Conversion Rate

Conversion Rate was created to understand how effectively generated leads were converted into customers. It helps move the analysis beyond simply counting leads and shows the quality and effectiveness of the conversion process.

![](https://github.com/siddiqmulla/IM-Packaging-Marketing-Analytics/blob/main/PowerBI/Screenshots/Conversion%20Rate.png)
---

## 6. Cost Per Lead (CPL)

Cost Per Lead helps measure the cost required to generate a marketing lead. This is useful for comparing the efficiency of different marketing activities and identifying where the company is spending its marketing budget more efficiently.

![](https://github.com/siddiqmulla/IM-Packaging-Marketing-Analytics/blob/main/PowerBI/Screenshots/Cost%20Per%20Lead.png)

---

## 7. ROI

ROI was created to compare the revenue generated with the marketing investment. This measure provides a financial view of campaign performance and helps understand the return obtained from the money spent on digital marketing.

![](https://github.com/siddiqmulla/IM-Packaging-Marketing-Analytics/blob/main/PowerBI/Screenshots/ROI.png)

---

## 8. Weekly Classification

A weekly classification was created from the campaign dates so that marketing performance could be viewed week by week. This makes it easier to identify changes in performance during the month and compare marketing activity across different weeks.

![](https://github.com/siddiqmulla/IM-Packaging-Marketing-Analytics/blob/main/PowerBI/Screenshots/Week.png)

---

## Overall Power BI Workflow

The Power BI analysis followed this workflow:

**Prepared Data → Data Model → DAX Measures → KPI Calculation → Weekly Analysis → Dashboard Visualisation**

The measures created in Power BI formed the calculation layer for the final dashboard. They were then used with charts, KPI cards, tables and filters to provide an interactive view of IM Packaging's marketing performance.

**Note:** The final Excel and Power BI dashboards are documented separately in the dashboard section of this project.
