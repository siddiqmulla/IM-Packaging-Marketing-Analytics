# IM-Packaging-Marketing-Analytics
MBA internship project on digital marketing analytics at IM Packaging using Python, SQL, Excel and Power BI.

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my MBA internship at **IM Packaging, Sangli**, where I worked as a **Marketing Analyst Intern**.

The project focuses on analysing digital marketing data to understand **leads, conversions, revenue, marketing spend, customer segments, geographical performance and campaign effectiveness**.

The complete analysis was carried out using **Python, MySQL, Microsoft Excel and Power BI**.

---

## Business Problem

IM Packaging uses different digital marketing channels to generate business enquiries. The main purpose of this project was to analyse marketing performance and understand:

- Which marketing channels are generating better results?
- Which cities are producing more conversions?
- Which customer segments are performing better?
- Which campaigns are generating higher results?
- How is marketing expenditure related to conversions?
- Can marketing data be used to predict conversion performance?

---

## Project Objectives

1. To evaluate the effectiveness of IM Packaging's digital marketing channels using marketing performance indicators.
2. To identify high-performing geographical markets and customer segments based on leads, conversions and revenue.
3. To examine the relationship between marketing expenditure and conversions and apply predictive analytics to understand conversion performance.
4. To evaluate advertisement performance through A/B testing and develop dashboards for data-driven marketing decision-making.

## Key Findings

- **WhatsApp** generated the highest number of leads and conversions, with **874 leads and 374 conversions**.
- **Kolhapur** recorded the highest number of conversions with **188**.
- **Packaging** recorded the highest number of conversions among the audience segments with **240**.
- The correlation between social media spend and conversions was very weak and statistically insignificant (**r = -0.0734, p = 0.6049**).
- The **Decision Tree** model performed better than the Linear Regression model for the analysed prediction task.
- **Leads** were the most important variable in the Decision Tree model, contributing approximately **84.47%** of feature importance.
- The A/B testing analysis showed a statistically significant difference between the two advertisement groups (**p = 0.00623**).

## Business Insights

The analysis showed that marketing performance varied across different channels, geographical markets, audience segments and individual campaigns.

WhatsApp generated the highest number of leads and conversions among the analysed channels, while Kolhapur recorded the highest number of conversions among the analysed cities. Packaging was the highest-converting audience segment.

The statistical analysis showed that marketing expenditure alone did not explain conversion performance in the analysed social media data. The very weak correlation between spend and conversions indicates the importance of considering other factors such as leads, customer segments, geographical markets and advertisement performance.

The predictive analysis further indicated that **leads were the most important feature** in the Decision Tree model. This highlights the importance of generating relevant and qualified leads rather than focusing only on increasing advertising expenditure.

The A/B testing analysis identified a statistically significant difference between the two advertisement groups, demonstrating the usefulness of testing different advertisement variations when evaluating campaign performance.
